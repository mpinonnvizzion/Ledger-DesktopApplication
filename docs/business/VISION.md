# Ledger Desktop Vision

## Our Vision

Ledger exists to give people complete ownership of their financial life.

Modern financial software has increasingly shifted toward subscription models, cloud dependency, and limited control over personal data. Many users are asked to trust third parties with sensitive financial information, pay recurring fees for basic functionality, or accept that their data lives on someone else's servers.

Ledger is built on a different philosophy.

We believe financial software should first serve its users — not the business model behind it.

Ledger is a professional desktop finance application that places privacy, ownership, performance, and reliability at the center of every decision. Users own their financial records, choose where their data is stored, and can continue using the application regardless of an internet connection or the status of any online service.

Our goal is not simply to replace spreadsheets or compete with existing finance applications. Our goal is to build software that users can rely on for years without feeling trapped by subscriptions, vendor lock-in, or unnecessary complexity.

---

## Our Mission

Build the most trusted local-first personal finance application for individuals, freelancers, and small businesses.

Every feature should help users better understand, organize, and manage their finances while respecting their privacy and ownership of their information.

---

## Core Principles

### Local First

User data belongs to the user.

Financial information should be stored locally by default, allowing complete ownership and control without requiring a cloud account.

### Privacy by Design

Privacy is a product feature, not a marketing slogan.

Ledger should collect as little information as possible and never require users to surrender ownership of their financial history to use the core application.

### Offline First

The application should remain fully functional without an internet connection.

Internet connectivity should enhance the experience — not define it.

Optional online services should never become mandatory for core financial management.

### Simplicity

Financial software should reduce stress rather than create it.

Ledger should emphasize intuitive workflows, thoughtful defaults, and clear organization over feature overload.

Every feature must justify its existence.

### Performance

Desktop software should feel immediate.

Pages should open quickly. Searching transactions should be instantaneous. Large datasets should remain responsive.

The application should feel native on every supported platform.

### Trust

Financial software must inspire confidence.

Users should always understand what the application is doing, why it is doing it, and how their information is being handled.

Transparency should guide every product decision.

### Longevity

Ledger is intended to become software that people trust for decades.

Long-term maintainability is more important than short-term convenience.

Every architectural and product decision should support sustainable growth.

## Who We Build For

Ledger is designed for people who value control over their finances without sacrificing usability.

Our primary customers include:

* Individuals managing personal finances.
* Freelancers tracking both personal and business expenses.
* Small business owners needing straightforward financial management.
* Privacy-conscious users who prefer local software over cloud-based alternatives.
* Professionals who want a fast, dependable finance application without unnecessary complexity.

---

## What Makes Ledger Different

Ledger is built around ownership instead of dependence.

Rather than encouraging recurring subscriptions for core functionality, Ledger provides a complete finance application through a one-time purchase.

Subscriptions are reserved only for services that incur recurring operational costs, such as bank synchronization through Plaid or future optional cloud services.

Users should never feel that basic financial management is being artificially restricted.

---

## What We Will Not Become

Ledger will not become software that:

* Requires a permanent internet connection.
* Stores user financial data by default in proprietary cloud infrastructure.
* Uses dark patterns to encourage subscriptions.
* Gates essential financial management behind recurring payments.
* Prioritizes marketing features over reliability.
* Sacrifices maintainability for rapid feature growth.

Every new feature should strengthen the product rather than increase complexity for its own sake.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Our ambition is to build the desktop finance application that users recommend because it earns their trust.

Success will not be measured solely by downloads or revenue, but by whether users feel confident that Ledger respects their data, saves them time, and remains dependable year after year.

If a user can install Ledger today and still confidently manage their finances with it ten years from now, we will have achieved our vision.
